What is color contrast ratio?

The contrast ratio measures how distinguishable text is from its background, from 1:1 (same color) to 21:1 (black on white). It is calculated from the relative luminance of both colors:

ratio = (Llighter + 0.05) / (Ldarker + 0.05)

WCAG 2.2 contrast requirements

LevelNormal textLarge text*
AA (standard)≥ 4.5 : 1≥ 3 : 1

* Large text = 18pt (24px) or larger, or 14pt (18.66px) bold or larger.

FAQ

What is a good color contrast ratio?

WCAG 2.2 requires at least 4.5:1 for normal text (AA) and 7:1 for AAA. Large text needs 3:1 (AA) / 4.5:1 (AAA).

How is the contrast ratio calculated?

From the relative luminance of each color: ratio = (L1 + 0.05) / (L2 + 0.05), with sRGB gamma correction exactly as WCAG defines it.

What is the difference between AA and AAA?

AA is the compliance level most laws and policies reference. AAA is stricter. ContrastLab grades both, for normal and large text.
